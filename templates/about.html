How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Add naira

Send a bank transfer to the account shown in the app, upload your payment screenshot and tap confirm. Your wallet is credited once a person has checked the payment.

2

Buy TCK

Swap naira for TCK at a fixed rate of ₦50 per TCK. Each swap has a 1% fee, and you see what you will receive before you confirm.

3

Send, save or vote

Pay friends by @username, lock TCK in a vault for rewards, or vote on community proposals.

What makes it different

Pay by @username

Send TCK to a friend with their username. No long wallet codes to copy, though you can use one if you want to.

One rate, one fee

TCK costs ₦50 and a swap costs 1%. No hidden charges, and the amount is shown before you confirm.

A person checks your money

Every deposit and withdrawal is reviewed by a person. Withdrawals need your 4-digit PIN and go only to the bank account saved on your profile.

The community decides

Members vote Yes or No on community proposals. Every account gets one vote on each proposal.

Everything on record

Your history and notifications show deposits, withdrawals and payments as they happen.

Save for rewards

Lock TCK in a 30 or 90 day vault to earn rewards at the rates shown in the app. Leaving early costs a 2% fee.

Community

Members have a say

Part of what TCK is for is giving members a voice. Proposals, such as funding grants for local developers or small businesses, are put to a vote. Every account has one vote on each proposal, and the results are shown in the app.

Safety and honesty

What we do, and what we don't promise.

What we do

  • Passwords and PINs are stored scrambled, never as plain text.
  • A person reviews every deposit and withdrawal.
  • Withdrawals need your PIN and go only to your saved bank account.
  • No fees on deposits, withdrawals or sending TCK to another member at the moment.

What we don't promise

  • We do not promise profit or returns. Rewards follow the rates shown in the app, and leaving a vault early costs a 2% fee.
  • TCK Token is not a bank.
  • Deposits and withdrawals are not instant, because a person checks them.
  • The rate of ₦50 per TCK is set by the platform. It is not a market price.
  • We will never ask for your password or PIN. Never share them with anyone.

Questions

What is TCK?

TCK is the balance you hold inside your TCK wallet. You buy it with naira in Swap at ₦50 per TCK, and you can sell it back the same way.

How do I add money?

Open Bank Cash-In, send a transfer to the account shown, upload your payment screenshot and tap I Confirm Payment. You get a notification when it is approved.

How do I withdraw?

Save your bank details on your Profile, open Bank Cash-In and choose Withdraw. The first time, you create a 4-digit PIN. The money is held while a person processes your request, and it goes back to your wallet if the request is declined.

Are there fees?

A 1% fee applies when you swap. We do not charge for deposits, withdrawals or sending TCK to another member at the moment.

How do I keep my account safe?

Use a strong password, keep your PIN private, and never share either with anyone, including us.
